STRESS MANAGEMENT GUIDE

Stress is a natural response of the body to challenges or demands. While a small amount of stress can be helpful, too much stress can affect both mental and physical health.

COMMON CAUSES OF STRESS:
- Academic or work pressure
- Financial problems
- Relationship issues
- Health concerns
- Lack of sleep

COMMON SYMPTOMS OF STRESS:
- Feeling overwhelmed or anxious
- Difficulty concentrating
- Headaches or body pain
- Changes in sleep or appetite
- Irritability or mood swings

EFFECTIVE STRESS MANAGEMENT TECHNIQUES:

1. Deep Breathing:
Take slow, deep breaths. Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 4 seconds. Repeat several times to calm your mind.

2. Physical Activity:
Regular exercise like walking, yoga, or stretching helps reduce stress hormones and improves mood.

3. Time Management:
Break tasks into smaller steps and prioritize important activities to avoid feeling overwhelmed.

4. Talk to Someone:
Sharing your thoughts with a friend, family member, or counselor can help relieve stress.

5. Mindfulness and Meditation:
Practicing mindfulness helps you stay present and reduces overthinking.

6. Healthy Lifestyle:
Maintain a balanced diet, get enough sleep, and avoid excessive caffeine or junk food.

7. Take Breaks:
Short breaks during work or study help refresh your mind and improve focus.

POSITIVE AFFIRMATIONS:
- I am capable of handling challenges.
- I am calm and in control.
- This feeling will pass.
- I deserve peace and happiness.

WHEN TO SEEK HELP:
If stress becomes overwhelming, lasts for a long time, or affects daily life, it is important to seek help from a mental health professional.
